# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

adaptive

## Users

Cargo and freight logistics managers & billing operators needing to collect shipment data, generate invoices, and log transactions.

## Product Purpose

Collect cargo data, itemize shipment charges (manual entry with custom rate calculations), generate exportable PDF invoices, and store invoice history securely in a database.

## Positioning

Streamlined, fast cargo invoicing tailored for logistics operations—combining itemized shipment data collection with instant PDF creation and database tracking across web and mobile platforms.

## Operating Context

Freight handling desks, warehouse management stations, and mobile field logistics operations where fast data entry, quick client billing, and immediate PDF invoice sharing are daily necessities.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- **Capabilities:** Manual cargo item entry, shipment charge calculations (weight, volume, distance), PDF export/download, database invoice history and status tracking.
- **Constraints:** Must adapt seamlessly between desktop web dashboards and native/mobile layout environments (touch targets, responsive navigation, native platform expectations).

## Brand Commitments

- Clean, high-trust, industrial-grade utility. Precise typography and high contrast for rapid data entry and document readability.

## Evidence on Hand

- Current repo contains minimal skeleton (`README.md`). Detailed visual design, schema, and UI tokens will be established as development proceeds.

## Product Principles

1. **Precision & Speed:** Streamline cargo data entry and invoice creation with minimal friction and zero visual noise.
2. **Platform Native & Adaptive:** Respect desktop web scanning/table density while providing touch-friendly 44pt/48pt targets and safe-area/navigation inset support on mobile devices.
3. **Clarity & Trust:** Output clear, professional, readable PDF invoices and billing statuses.
