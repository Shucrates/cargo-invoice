# Design System: Rudra Cargo & Transport NX

## Overview

**Creative Theme: "Japanese Indigo & Industrial Charcoal"**

The Rudra Cargo & Transport NX terminal is built around an executive industrial aesthetic. It combines high-density data legibility with a curated palette featuring Japanese Indigo, Charcoal, Steel Teal, Mid Grey, Foggy, and Star Dust neutrals.

---

## Color Palette

The official brand color system consists of 6 core color families, each specified with 6 precise tonal hex swatches:

### 1. Charcoal (Primary Dark & Neutral Base)
Used for primary dark headers, high-contrast text, dark mode containers, and structural depth.

- `#000000` — Deep Pitch Black
- `#090909` — Deep Surface Shadow
- `#111111` — Primary Dark Terminal Header Background
- `#232323` — Primary Dark Surface / Card Header
- `#2C2C2C` — Secondary Dark Neutral
- `#353535` — Muted Dark Border / Card Accent

### 2. Japanese Indigo (Primary Brand Accent)
The signature brand color for primary actions, active navigation tabs, interactive buttons, and brand highlights.

- `#16303D` — Deepest Indigo Base
- `#193746` — Dark Indigo Surface
- `#1C3E4E` — **Primary Brand Indigo** (Buttons, Selected Tabs, Primary Badges)
- `#224D5F` — Interactive Hover State
- `#255468` — Interactive Active State
- `#285C70` — Bright Indigo Highlight / Accent Gradient

### 3. Steel Teal (Secondary Brand Accent)
Used for secondary actions, export buttons, status highlights, and interactive tooltips.

- `#3F5A66` — Dark Steel Teal
- `#486675` — Medium Steel Teal
- `#517380` — Soft Steel Teal
- `#60899B` — **Secondary Brand Accent** (Export Buttons, Action Bars)
- `#6692A4` — Light Steel Teal
- `#6C9BAD` — Highlighting & Active Badges

### 4. Mid Grey (Structural Borders & Muted Text)
Used for form input strokes, table grid borders, divider lines, and muted secondary text.

- `#757575` — Muted Body Text
- `#7E7E7E` — Medium Slate Neutral
- `#808080` — Standard Table & Card Border
- `#989898` — Light Slate Divider
- `#A1A1A1` — Input Strokes & Border Outlines
- `#AAAAAA` — Soft Outline & Placeholder Text

### 5. Foggy (Warm Soft Neutral / Section Accents)
Used for subtle background section fills, consignor/consignee container backgrounds, and warm table highlights.

- `#9E9C8A` — Deep Foggy Accent
- `#A8A692` — Medium Foggy
- `#B1AF9A` — Soft Muted Olive / Fog
- `#C4C2AA` — Light Foggy Tint
- `#CDCAB2` — Soft Surface Background
- `#D6D3BA` — Warm Light Neutral Highlight

### 6. Star Dust (Light Page Canvas & Card Surfaces)
The primary light theme canvas background, form card surfaces, and paper invoice preview background.

- `#CECAC8` — Cool Light Grey Border
- `#D8D3D1` — Soft Sand Divider
- `#E2DDDA` — Light Surface Neutral
- `#F2EDEA` — **Main Page Canvas Background**
- `#F9F3F1` — Form Card Surface Background
- `#FFFAF8` — High-Luminance Input & Paper Surface
